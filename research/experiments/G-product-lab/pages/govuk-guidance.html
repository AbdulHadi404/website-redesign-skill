Apply for or renew a Blue Badge

You can apply for a Blue Badge if you have a disability or health condition that affects your mobility.

You can also apply for a badge if you care for a child with a health condition or you're applying on behalf of someone else.

It costs up to £10 in England, £20 in Scotland and is free in Wales.

Start now

Before you start

You'll need:

  • a recent digital photo showing your head and shoulders
  • a photo or scan of your proof of identity
  • a photo or scan of your proof of address
  • your National Insurance number (if you have one)
  • details of your current Blue Badge (if you're reapplying)
Your council may ask for other information, for example if you get benefits.

How long it takes

Applications usually take around 12 weeks. It may take longer if you do not provide all the information needed.

If your badge is lost or stolen

You can report a lost or stolen badge to your council.

Help with your application
Contact your council if you need help applying.
! WarningYou can be fined up to £1,000 if you do not return a badge when asked.
