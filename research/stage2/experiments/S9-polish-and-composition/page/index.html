How a week gets planned

Three steps, usually done on a Friday afternoon, instead of a Sunday night with a spreadsheet.

Copy last week

Start from the rota that worked, then move the shifts that change. Holidays and days off are already blocked out.

Watch the numbers

Each shift you place updates hours per person and the week’s wage bill, so the budget is checked while you plan.

Publish to phones

Staff get a notification with their own shifts. Swaps come back to you to approve, and the rota updates for everyone.

“A rota is a budget you can read. We built Rotabell so the person writing it can see what it costs.”

From the Rotabell team

What your staff see

Their own shifts for the next two weeks, with the address and who else is on. Nothing to install: a link opens it in the phone’s browser.
