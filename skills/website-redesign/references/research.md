# Phase 2 — Research

Goal: calibrate what "finished" looks like in this company's world, and extract principles you can apply to *their* brand. The output is a short list of principles, not a mood board to copy.

## What to open

Aim for 4–8 sites, chosen deliberately:

- The reference the user named, if any. Treat it as a quality bar, never a template.
- Two or three direct competitors or the closest adjacent category (if the company sells voice agents, look at the best voice-AI sites; if it sells accounting software, the best fintech sites).
- One or two best-in-class sites outside the category whose *problem* is similar (a technical product that must show its UI; a service business that must build trust without a product screenshot; a physical product that lives on photography).
- One site that is deliberately different in tone from the obvious choice, so the direction is chosen rather than defaulted.

Actually load them. Screenshot the first viewport and, where possible, one or two lower sections. Sites that render on scroll may capture blank below the fold — page text extraction still tells you the structure.

## What to note, per site

Write two or three lines each. Look for:

- **Headline**: how many words, what size relative to the viewport, serif or grotesk, how the emphasis is handled.
- **First viewport**: what fills it — photography, product, typography, a demo — and how much of it is empty.
- **Type scale**: how many distinct sizes are visible on the homepage, and whether body copy is comfortable.
- **Surfaces**: how sections change (colour, full-bleed images, rules), and whether cards exist at all.
- **Product**: shown as real UI, abstracted fragments, or not at all — and at what size.
- **Imagery**: role (narrative, environment, proof, texture) and treatment (colour, duotone, black and white).
- **Proof**: how customers, numbers and testimonials are presented, and how sparingly.
- **Motion**: what moves on load and on scroll, and whether it carries meaning.
- **Restraint**: what they chose *not* to do.

## What to extract

Turn the notes into principles that are independent of any one site's look, for example:

- "One primary action, repeated; secondary actions are text links."
- "The product appears large and early; features are described next to the screen that runs them."
- "Photography is of the customer's world, treated consistently, and always paired with a solid surface for copy."
- "Chapters alternate surfaces so the page has visible structure."
- "Display type does the branding; UI type stays neutral."

Record in `DESIGN.md` (section "References") what was taken from each site *conceptually* and, just as important, what was deliberately not taken. A redesign that names its influences is easier for the user to trust and easier to keep original.

## Without a browser

If no browser or fetch capability is available, say so in the report and in `DESIGN.md`, name what would help (the built-in preview browser or a browser MCP), and proceed on the audit plus your knowledge of the category. Do not pretend references were inspected.
