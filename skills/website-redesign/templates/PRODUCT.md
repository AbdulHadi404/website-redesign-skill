## The configurable thing (if any)

- **Model:** every variable, its options, and its dependencies. This is the single source of truth for
  preview, summary, estimate, messages and the stored record.
- **Flow:** a few chapters, not a field list. What is asked, what is inferred, and what only
  appears conditionally.
- **Preview:** how the customer sees their choices, and how honestly it is labelled.
- **Estimate:** the formula, what makes it refuse to show a number, and how it is worded.
- **Starting from an example:** which parts stay fixed and which are editable.
